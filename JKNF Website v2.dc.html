Our Philosophy

सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः

Sarve Bhavantu Sukhinaḥ · May all be happy

The line carried beneath the lotus is a Sanskrit invocation for the wellbeing of all. It sets the standard the work is measured against: support given without condition, to whoever needs it, so that wellbeing is shared rather than selective.

The Foundation

A charitable foundation dedicated to serving underserved communities through meaningful, need-based support — with a focus on preserving dignity and creating lasting impact.

What we do

Ten initiatives spanning education, healthcare, disability support, child welfare, senior care, nutrition and social assistance. Each addresses one clearly identified need.

Who we serve

JKNF Drishti

Vision & spectacle initiative

Conduct eye-screening and vision-check camps for underserved communities, with spectacles provided to beneficiaries who require them and referrals for further treatment where necessary.

Potential brand donors: ZEISS · Titan Eye+ · Lenskart

Education support initiative

Provide direct educational assistance to deserving students, including school/college fees, books, uniforms, examination expenses and other essential educational requirements.

Potential brand donors: Navneet · DOMS Industries · Kokuyo Camlin
03

JKNF Saksham

Mobility & assistive support initiative

Support persons with disabilities through artificial limbs, wheelchairs, crutches, tricycles and other mobility or assistive devices that improve independence and dignity.

Potential brand donors: Sakata Inx India · Resostoflex · Ultra International
04

JKNF Aarogya Sahayata

Medical assistance programme

Provide financial assistance to individuals from economically weaker backgrounds facing serious medical conditions, supporting treatment, diagnostics, medicines or hospital expenses.

Potential brand donors: Dr. Lal PathLabs · Metropolis Healthcare · Apollo Pharmacy
05

JKNF Aashray

Vulnerable children support programme

Support vulnerable and orphaned children with education, nutrition, school fees, books, uniforms and other essential requirements to help them continue their education and live with dignity.

Potential brand donors: Parle Products · Britannia Industries · Tata Consumer Products
06

JKNF Saath

Senior care support initiative

Support senior citizens living in care homes through nutrition, medicines, healthcare assistance, personal-care supplies and other basic necessities.

Potential brand donors: Dabur India · Emami · Centum Electronics
07

JKNF Annapurna

Community nutrition & seva initiative

Organise nutritious meal and essential-food distribution initiatives for underserved communities, combining direct relief with volunteer-led community service.

Potential brand donors: Haldiram’s · Bikaji Foods · Tata Consumer Products
08

JKNF Shiksha

School essentials & infrastructure initiative

Address one clearly identified need at a school or educational institution—such as desks, benches, books, learning materials or classroom equipment—through direct sponsorship or in-kind support.

Potential brand donors: Nilkamal · Supreme Industries · Navneet
09

JKNF Scholarship Circle

Madhusudan Narayan scholarship programme

Create a focused scholarship programme for deserving students from financially disadvantaged backgrounds, providing meaningful annual educational support and potentially developing into JKNF’s signature education initiative.

Potential brand donors: HDFC Life · Bajaj Allianz Life · SBI Life
10

JKNF Dignity at Home

Independent living support

Help persons with disabilities live more independently by supporting mobility equipment, essential assistive devices and practical modifications or support required at home.

Potential brand donors: Godrej Enterprises Group · Panasonic Life Solutions India · Häfele India

The centenary of seva

100 Lives, One Legacy

Celebrating the Birth Centenary of Late Shri Madhusudan Narayan

The Centenary Celebration will bring together 100 individuals whose lives have been positively impacted through the Foundation’s 10 initiatives, representing education, healthcare, disability support, child welfare, senior care, nutrition and social relief. The evening will also honour the partners, organisations and businesses from Bihar who came forward in support of these noble causes. Designed as an intimate evening of gratitude and recognition, it places the beneficiaries and the spirit of seva at its centre.

Suggested event treatment
  • A small, intimate evening focused on gratitude, recognition and impact.
  • Highlight 100 beneficiaries through 100 short stories, portraits or impact moments.
  • Recognise the Bihar-based partners, organisations and businesses who supported the drives.
  • Keep the evening service-led and heritage-led rather than a conventional awards gala.
  • Use the centenary as the culmination of the 10-activity portfolio and the continuation of the founder’s legacy.
Our approach

Four tests every programme has to pass.

Applied before a drive is run, and again before anything about it is published.

01

Dignity

People are shown as participants, never as subjects of need. No pity framing in images or copy, and no photograph published without consent.

02

Need-based

Every claim names the specific support given — spectacles fitted, fees paid, devices provided. Concrete numbers over general sentiment.

03

Continuity

A legacy carried since 2006. Programmes run as continuing commitments rather than seasonal appeals, so the same community can rely on them.

04

Restraint

One clearly identified need at a time, met in full and reported the same way. Nothing more is claimed than what was done.
