Corporate partnerships

Fund one identified need, and see exactly where it went.

We work with companies, foundations and family offices on CSR commitments, in-kind supply and employee-led volunteering across ten initiatives.

Ways to partner

Four routes, all tied to a named programme.

01

CSR funding

Sponsor an initiative for a financial year, or a single drive within it, against a defined scope and budget.

02

In-kind supply

Spectacles, assistive devices, books, uniforms, desks, medicines or provisions supplied directly to a programme.

03

Employee volunteering

Teams join screening camps, meal distribution and school drives, with the Foundation handling logistics on the ground.

04

Scholarship endowment

Endow annual scholarships through the Madhusudan Narayan Scholarship Circle, in your name or the Foundation's.

How a partnership runs
01 · Scope

We agree the initiative, the geography and the number of beneficiaries the commitment covers.

02 · Agreement

An MoU records the scope, the schedule, the reporting format and the compliance documents exchanged.

03 · Delivery

The drive is run by the Foundation, with your team invited to attend or take part on site.

04 · Report

A closing report names the specific support given, with photographs and utilisation details.

Where partners fit

Each initiative has a natural industry alongside it.

Optical and eyewear for Drishti; stationery and publishing for Vidya and Shiksha; mobility and assistive manufacturing for Saksham and Dignity at Home; diagnostics and pharmacy for Aarogya Sahayata; food and nutrition for Annapurna and Aashray; insurance and financial services for the Scholarship Circle; consumer care for Saath.
