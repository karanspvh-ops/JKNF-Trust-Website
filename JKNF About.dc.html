About the Foundation

An institution built to outlast the people who run it.

A public charitable trust in Patna, serving underserved communities since 2006 through meaningful, need-based support — governed by its trustees and answerable for every rupee it receives.

The legacy

Carried in the name of Late Shri Madhusudan Narayan.

Founded in 2006, the Foundation carries forward his philanthropic legacy. The work is deliberately formal and consistent rather than seasonal: programmes run as continuing commitments, so the same community can rely on them year after year.

His birth centenary is marked not with a gala, but by bringing together one hundred people the Foundation's work has reached.

The Foundation

A charitable foundation dedicated to serving underserved communities through meaningful, need-based support — with a focus on preserving dignity and creating lasting impact.

Mandate

Ten initiatives across education, healthcare, disability support, child welfare, senior care, nutrition and social assistance. Each addresses one clearly identified need.

Reach

Underserved communities in and around Patna — students, patients, persons with disabilities, vulnerable children and senior citizens.

Constitution

A public charitable trust registered in Patna under the Indian Trust Act 1882, governed by its Board of Trustees.

Funding

Individual donations, corporate CSR commitments and in-kind supply, each directed to the programme the donor names.

How the Foundation is run
01

Trustee oversight

Programmes, commitments and expenditure sit with the Board of Trustees, not with any single office holder.

02

Compliance

Registration, PAN and statutory records are maintained and shared with partners on request.

03

Directed giving

A contribution goes to the initiative the donor names. Nothing is quietly reallocated.

04

Plain reporting

Each drive is reported by what it actually provided. Concrete numbers over general sentiment.

Governance

Board of Trustees

The trustees hold responsibility for the Foundation's programmes, its compliance and its long-term direction.
